Gave stateless-svc an optional startup probe, for Arranger's search server, which waits up to about ten minutes for its search engine before it listens.

- `stateless-svc/templates/deployment.yaml`, `values.yaml`, `Chart.yaml`, `MIGRATION.md`: `probes.startup` renders a startup probe on the container port when set, with `path` required and the other fields left to Kubernetes' defaults when absent; 1.7.1, since a release without it renders as on 1.7.0
- `stateless-svc/tests/probes_test.yaml`: no probe by default, the fields given, the fields left out, and a refusal without `path`
